# Daily Session Operating System

## Standard 60-minute session

### 0–10 min — Retrieval
- Review yesterday's vocabulary without notes.
- Produce each item in a sentence.
- Recycle 5 older items from 3–7 days ago.
- Mark failures, do not immediately relearn everything.

### 10–20 min — Grammar as production
- Work only on the week's target structure.
- Say 10 original sentences aloud.
- Write 5 more from your own life.
- Transform at least 3: statement → question, present → past, affirmative → negative, or main clause → subordinate clause.

### 20–30 min — Input
Alternate listening and reading by day.
- First pass: gist, no pausing.
- Second pass: identify 5 useful phrases.
- Third pass only if needed: inspect difficult lines.

### 30–45 min — Speaking
- 3 min shadowing or pronunciation.
- 5 min guided response to the week's topic.
- 5 min spontaneous response without notes.
- 2 min correction/retry: correct one high-value error and repeat the sentence/task immediately.

### 45–55 min — Writing / transfer
Write 50–120 words depending on level, or complete a practical form/message/task. Use the week's grammar and at least 5 target words.

### 55–60 min — Log
Record:
- new items attempted;
- retrieval %;
- one pronunciation problem;
- one grammar error;
- one phrase worth keeping;
- tomorrow's carry-over.

## 30-minute emergency version
- 5 min vocabulary retrieval
- 5 min grammar sentences
- 7 min listening/reading
- 10 min speaking
- 3 min log

Never replace speaking with passive study.

## Monday–Saturday functional rhythm

**Monday — Build:** new topic, vocabulary, grammar pattern, controlled speaking.  
**Tuesday — Stabilize:** retrieval, pronunciation, listening, sentence transformations.  
**Wednesday — Speak:** conversation-heavy day, role-play and real-life phrases.  
**Thursday — Read + write:** reading comprehension, short written output, grammar accuracy.  
**Friday — Transfer:** authentic/Super Easy German input, Funo conversation, real-world mission preparation.  
**Saturday — Test + repair:** no major new grammar. Weekly quiz, recorded speaking, writing, vocabulary test and repair.

## Correction rule

Do not correct every error during speaking. Prioritize one error that most improves intelligibility or the week's target structure. Correct it, explain briefly, then immediately repeat the same communicative act correctly.

## Shadowing protocol
1. Listen once without speaking.
2. Read transcript if available.
3. Listen sentence-by-sentence.
4. Copy rhythm and stress, not just consonants.
5. Record yourself for 30–60 seconds.
6. Compare once.
7. Repeat the most problematic line three times.

## Vocabulary retrieval protocol
An item is active only if you can:
1. understand it in context;
2. recall it from meaning/topic cue;
3. say a natural phrase/sentence with it;
4. retrieve it again after at least 48 hours.

## Saturday scoring
Every Saturday score 100 points:
- Vocabulary 20
- Grammar 20
- Listening/reading 20
- Speaking/pronunciation 25
- Writing 15

70 = pass. 80 = strong. 90+ = advance material may be added. Under 70 triggers targeted repair in the following week.

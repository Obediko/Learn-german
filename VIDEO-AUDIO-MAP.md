# Verified Video & Audio Map

This file is the media layer for the six-month course. Use media to train listening, pronunciation and conversational transfer—not as passive entertainment.

## Core verified resources

### EG-A1 — Easy German A1: For Absolute Beginners
https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLk1fjOl39-50kWobutO8NVFzbw9PHtbbg
Use in Weeks 1–13. This is the default beginner video bank.

### EG-A2 — Easy German A2: For Advanced Beginners
https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLk1fjOl39-5201BUdhtOM_x23poNvLouT
Use in Weeks 14–26.

### EG-SUPER — Super Easy German
https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLk1fjOl39-53GxQIn1Hxdouokf0J0SDpl
Use whenever normal Easy German is too fast. Prefer this for shadowing in Months 1–2.

### GOETHE-A1 — Goethe A1 official practice
https://www.goethe.de/ins/de/en/prf/prf/gzsd1/ueb.html
Contains official listening, reading, writing and speaking practice. Use in Weeks 6, 12 and 13.

### GOETHE-A2 — Goethe A2 official practice
https://www.goethe.de/ins/de/de/m/prf/prf/gzsd2/ub2.html
Use from Week 19 and intensively in Weeks 25–26.

### GOETHE-ELEMENTARY — Goethe A1/A2 life-in-Germany practice
https://www.goethe.de/de/spr/ueb/ele.html
Use for practical themes such as shopping, doctor visits and train travel.

### GOETHE-MWD — Mein Weg nach Deutschland
https://www.goethe.de/prj/mwd/en/deu.html
Includes A1 videos/exercises and A1–B2 workplace German. Use for real-life Germany transfer.

### VHS-A1/A2 — VHS-Lernportal Deutsch
https://www.vhs-lernportal.de/
Use for high-volume structured exercises when a grammar/vocabulary pattern needs extra repetitions.

### FUNO — FunoGerman
Use the live app for personalized conversation, pronunciation feedback, correction-retry loops and weak-area recycling. The sequence must follow the FunoGerman master curriculum rather than random app exploration.

## Media protocol

For a normal media block:
1. First pass: listen/watch without pausing. Write one-sentence gist.
2. Second pass: capture 5 useful phrases, not isolated words.
3. Third pass: shadow 30–90 seconds.
4. Close the video and retell the content in German for 60–180 seconds.
5. Use at least 3 extracted phrases in your own sentences.

Do not spend more than 15–20 minutes of a 60-minute session consuming media. The purpose is output.

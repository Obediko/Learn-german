# Assessments and Mastery Gates

## Weekly Saturday score: 100 points
- Vocabulary retrieval: 20
- Grammar production: 20
- Listening/reading: 20
- Speaking/pronunciation: 25
- Writing: 15

### Interpretation
90–100: excellent; add optional stretch material.
80–89: strong; continue normally.
70–79: pass; continue with one repair block.
60–69: weak pass; continue only if no two domains are below 50%.
<60: repeat targeted material before adding full new load.

## Speaking rubric: 25 points
- task completion 5
- intelligibility/pronunciation 5
- grammar control 5
- vocabulary range 4
- fluency 4
- repair/interaction 2

## Writing rubric: 15 points
- task completion 3
- organization 3
- grammar 4
- vocabulary 3
- spelling/capitalization 2

## Checkpoint 1 — Week 6, A1.1
Must be able to:
- introduce self spontaneously;
- ask/answer personal questions;
- order simple food/items;
- describe routine and family;
- write a short message;
- understand slow everyday exchanges.
Pass: >=70 overall and speaking >=14/25.

## Checkpoint 2 — Week 13, A1 exit
Must demonstrate:
- present tense + common irregular verbs;
- modal verbs;
- nominative/accusative foundations and common dative chunks;
- separable verbs;
- negation/questions;
- time/date;
- basic Perfekt and war/hatte;
- weil sentence;
- 5-minute practical conversation;
- 80–100 word message.
Pass: >=75 overall, no domain <55.

## Checkpoint 3 — Week 19, A2.1
Must demonstrate:
- connected biography/past narration;
- housing comparison;
- complaint/service interaction;
- future plans with reasons;
- 5–7 minute conversation;
- 100–130 word text.
Pass: >=75 overall.

## Checkpoint 4 — Week 25, A2 mock
Use Goethe-style task conditions: timed listening/reading, practical writing and speaking simulation.
Pass target: >=75 overall and speaking >=18/25.

## Final — Week 26
Requirements:
- vocabulary delayed recall >=80%;
- listening/reading >=75%;
- speaking >=20/25;
- writing >=12/15;
- reliable Perfekt;
- reliable verb-final weil/dass/wenn in familiar contexts;
- practical formal email;
- 10-minute conversation with repair strategies.

## B1 bridge decision
Advance immediately to B1.1 only if:
- final >=80/100;
- no domain <65%;
- speaking >=20/25;
- writing >=12/15;
- A2 grammar can be used spontaneously, not only recognized.

## Failure-recovery rule
Do not repeat whole levels because of one weakness.
If vocabulary fails: reduce new items 30% and add retrieval.
If grammar fails: 10 transformations/day for the failed structure.
If listening fails: easier audio + three-pass protocol for 7 days.
If speaking fails: add 10 minutes speaking daily; scripts only for first attempt.
If writing fails: rewrite one corrected text twice during the next week.

# Vocabulary System

## Target
- Weeks 1–8: 12 new active items per study day.
- Weeks 9–26: 10 new active items per study day.
- Saturday: no large new list. Retrieval, sorting and phrase repair.

An item can be a word or a useful chunk. Prefer chunks whenever possible.

## What to record
For nouns: article + singular + plural + one phrase. Example: `die Wohnung, -en | eine ruhige Wohnung`.
For verbs: infinitive + present form + relevant past form + one sentence. Example: `fahren | fährt | ist gefahren | Ich bin mit der U-Bahn gefahren.`
For adjectives: adjective + opposite/contrast + one natural sentence.
For chunks: learn as one unit, e.g. `Könnten Sie das bitte wiederholen?`

## Retrieval schedule
Every new item is tested:
- same day after 10–20 minutes;
- next day;
- 3 days later;
- 7 days later;
- 14 days later;
- 30 days later.

Do not mark an item active until it survives at least one delayed retrieval after 48 hours.

## Daily protocol
1. Retrieve yesterday's list without looking.
2. Say every successful item in a sentence.
3. Add today's 10–12 items from the weekly topic.
4. Produce 5 mini-dialogue lines using the new material.
5. End the day by recalling the items in random order.

## Weekly vocabulary test
Saturday test 30 items:
- 10 from current week;
- 10 from previous two weeks;
- 10 older items.

Scoring:
- 1 point = understands only;
- 2 points = recalls form;
- 3 points = uses correctly in a sentence.
Aim for at least 80% of available points.

## Topic sequence
Weeks 1–5: identity, study/work, hobbies, food, time/routine, family.
Weeks 7–11: housing, city/transport, health, shopping, celebrations.
Weeks 14–18: education, housing search, services/complaints, plans, culture/leisure.
Weeks 20–24: university administration, applications, money, transport disruption, travel.
Weeks 25–26: cumulative high-frequency A1/A2 repair.

## Phrase-first rule
At least 40% of all new items after Week 6 should be multi-word chunks, not isolated dictionary entries.

Priority chunks include:
- `Ich hätte gern ...`
- `Könnten Sie ...?`
- `Ich verstehe nicht ganz.`
- `Wie meinen Sie das?`
- `Das hängt davon ab.`
- `Meiner Meinung nach ...`
- `Ich bin damit einverstanden.`
- `Ich habe gestern ... gemacht.`
- `Ich interessiere mich für ...`
- `Ich möchte mich über ... informieren.`

## Anti-cramming rule
If daily delayed-recall accuracy falls below 70% for three consecutive study days, reduce new items by 30% until retrieval returns above 80%.

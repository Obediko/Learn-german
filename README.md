# Learn German — Six-Month Intensive System

**Dates:** 14 September 2026 to 13 March 2027  
**Study rhythm:** Monday–Saturday, 45–60 minutes minimum. Sunday = rest/light exposure only.  
**Core target:** secure A2 across speaking, listening, reading and writing, with a B1.1 bridge only if final mastery evidence supports it.  
**Curriculum spine:** FunoGerman master curriculum, itself grounded in Kurs DaF A1/A2 and CEFR/Goethe outcomes.

## What this repository is

This is a complete self-directed German school, not a vocabulary list, grammar checklist or app-streak plan. It is designed to move German from recognition to actual use in Germany.

The course has **26 weeks × 6 study days = 156 day-level lesson cards**. Every week combines:
1. active vocabulary;
2. pronunciation;
3. grammar in sentences;
4. listening;
5. reading;
6. speaking;
7. writing;
8. retrieval/revision;
9. a real-world task;
10. a measurable checkpoint.

## Six-month architecture

- **Weeks 1–6:** A1.1 foundations
- **Weeks 7–13:** A1.2 and A1 exit gate
- **Weeks 14–19:** A2.1
- **Weeks 20–25:** A2.2
- **Week 26:** A2 final assessment + B1 bridge decision

## Daily non-negotiables

On a normal 60-minute day:
- 10 min vocabulary retrieval
- 10 min grammar/pattern production
- 10 min listening or reading
- 15 min speaking/pronunciation
- 10 min writing or transfer task
- 5 min error log + tomorrow's carry-over

At least 20 minutes must involve active production. Watching videos or tapping multiple-choice answers does not count as speaking practice.

## Vocabulary target

Target **12 new active items per study day** for the first eight weeks, then **10 per day** as phrases become longer and grammar more complex. This yields roughly 1,500–1,700 encountered items across the six months, but raw count is not the objective.

An item is active only when you can retrieve it from a topic/meaning cue and use it in a natural sentence without seeing the English answer. Every noun is learned with article + plural where useful. Every verb is learned in a phrase and, once past tense begins, with its relevant past form.

## Advancement rule

A week is passed when:
- weekly score is at least 70/100;
- speaking reaches the minimum weekly threshold;
- vocabulary retrieval is at least 80% on the active set;
- the required writing task is completed;
- the real-world or simulated transfer task is attempted.

One weak domain does not force repetition of an entire week. Use `RECOVERY.md` to carry only the weak skill forward. Two or more major domains below 50% trigger bridge behavior before new heavy grammar is added.

## Success after six months

A strong realistic outcome is that you can handle routine life in Germany, sustain several-minute conversations on familiar topics, understand clear everyday speech, read practical texts, write connected messages/emails, narrate recent events, explain reasons and plans, solve common practical problems and enter B1 study without fragile A1 gaps.

## Golden rule

Do not measure progress by videos watched, pages completed or app streaks. Measure what you can retrieve, understand, say, write and do in German without a script.

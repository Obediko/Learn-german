# Learn German — Six-Month Intensive System

**Dates:** 14 September 2026 to 13 March 2027  
**Study rhythm:** Monday–Saturday, 45–60 minutes minimum. Sunday = rest/light exposure only.  
**Core target:** secure A2 across speaking, listening, reading and writing, with a B1.1 bridge if monthly mastery gates are passed early.  
**Curriculum spine:** FunoGerman master curriculum, itself grounded in Kurs DaF A1/A2 and CEFR/Goethe outcomes.

## Why this course exists

This is not a grammar checklist and not an app-streak plan. It is a six-month deliberate-practice system designed to move German from recognition to actual use in Germany.

Every week combines:
1. active vocabulary;
2. pronunciation;
3. grammar in sentences;
4. listening;
5. reading;
6. speaking;
7. writing;
8. retrieval/revision;
9. a real-world task;
10. a measurable checkpoint.

6. Other resources only when they add something the core sources do not.

## Six-month architecture

- **Weeks 1–6:** A1.1 foundations
- **Weeks 7–13:** A1.2 and A1 exit gate
- **Weeks 14–19:** A2.1
- **Weeks 20–25:** A2.2
- **Week 26:** A2 final assessment + B1 bridge decision

## Daily non-negotiables

On a normal 60-minute day:
- 10 min vocabulary retrieval
- 10 min grammar/pattern production
- 10 min listening or reading
- 15 min speaking/pronunciation
- 10 min writing or transfer task
- 5 min error log + tomorrow's carry-over

At least 20 minutes must involve active production. Watching videos or tapping multiple-choice answers does not count as speaking practice.

## Vocabulary target

Target **12 new active items per study day** for the first eight weeks, then **10 per day** as phrases become longer and grammar more complex. This produces roughly 1,500–1,700 encountered items over six months, but the goal is not raw count. An item is considered active only if you can retrieve it in a sentence without seeing the English prompt.

Every noun is learned with article + plural where useful. Every verb is learned with one natural sentence and, when relevant, its past form. Prefer chunks such as `Ich hätte gern ...`, `Könnten Sie ...?`, `Ich interessiere mich für ...` over isolated words.

## Advancement rule

A week is passed when:
- weekly score is at least 70/100;
- speaking is at least 14/20;
- vocabulary retrieval is at least 80% on the week's active set;
- one written product is completed;
- one real-world or simulated transfer task is attempted.

If one domain is weak, continue to the next week but carry a repair block forward. Do not repeat an entire week unless two or more domains score below 50%.

## Success after six months

A realistic strong outcome is that you can handle routine life in Germany, sustain several-minute conversations on familiar topics, understand clear everyday speech, read practical texts, write connected messages/emails, narrate recent events in the past, give reasons and opinions, solve common problems, and begin B1 work without fragile A1 gaps.

# German Grammar Spiral — A1 to Strong A2

Grammar is introduced, revisited under new vocabulary, and then tested under spontaneous production. A structure is not considered mastered after one week.

## Spiral principles
1. Introduce in a concrete communicative context.
2. Practise controlled production.
3. Reuse under a different topic within 1–3 weeks.
4. Test in spontaneous speaking/writing.
5. Recycle after 4–8 weeks.

## Sequence
### Weeks 1–6: sentence foundations
- personal pronouns: ich/du/Sie/er/sie
- sein, haben, heißen, wohnen, kommen
- regular present tense
- W-questions and yes/no questions
- basic verb-second order
- negation with nicht/kein
- accusative foundations
- möchten/nehmen
- separable verbs and sentence bracket
- possessives

### Weeks 7–13: functional A1 system
- es gibt + accusative
- common dative chunks and location phrases
- two-way-preposition awareness
- modal verbs: können, müssen, sollen, wollen
- imperative chunks and polite requests
- Perfekt with haben/sein; frequent irregular participles
- Präteritum chunks: war/hatte
- comparisons
- weil + verb-final
- cumulative nominative/accusative/dative recognition and production

### Weeks 14–19: connected A2 production
- productive Perfekt
- Präteritum of sein/haben/modals
- temporal connectors
- comparative and superlative
- polite requests: könnte / würde gern
- werden + Infinitiv
- vorhaben / wollen
- dass / weil review
- wenn clauses
- personal pronouns across cases in high-frequency patterns
- reflexive verbs in everyday contexts

### Weeks 20–26: practical A2 independence
- indirect-question chunks
- formal email syntax
- subordinate-clause accuracy under pressure
- deshalb / trotzdem / denn distinction in use
- verb-preposition combinations
- two-way prepositions revisited productively
- adjective endings at functional A2 level in high-frequency noun phrases
- infinitive + zu in frequent patterns where useful
- pronoun reference and sentence cohesion
- word order with time/manner/place and subordinate clauses

## Recycling map
Every major structure must appear in at least three phases:
- V2 word order: Weeks 1–6 → 7–13 → 20+
- cases: Weeks 3/7 → 10/15 → 20+
- modal verbs: Weeks 9 → 11/17 → 23+
- Perfekt: Week 8 → 14 → 23–26
- weil/dass/wenn: Weeks 11 → 17/18 → 21–26
- comparative: Week 10 → 15 → 18/24

## Mastery test
A grammar target is functionally reliable only if the learner can:
- produce 8/10 controlled examples correctly;
- use it in a 2+ minute spontaneous speaking task;
- use it in a written task without prompting;
- retain it at a later checkpoint.

Recognition alone does not count as mastery.

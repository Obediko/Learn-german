# German Listening Ladder — 26 Weeks

Listening difficulty increases deliberately. The goal is not endless exposure; it is measurable comprehension under gradually more natural speech.

## Phase 1 — Weeks 1–4
Target: slow learner speech and highly predictable topics.
- approximate speech rate: 70–100 wpm
- subtitles allowed only after first pass
- first-pass target: identify topic + 3 facts
- second-pass target: 70%+ detail comprehension

## Phase 2 — Weeks 5–8
Target: slow natural speech and short authentic exchanges.
- first pass without English subtitles
- German subtitles/transcript after second pass
- extract max 5 reusable phrases
- shadow 30–60 seconds

## Phase 3 — Weeks 9–13
Target: controlled authentic everyday German.
- routine service interactions
- short street interviews
- transport/health/shopping scenarios
- first-pass target: 60–70% gist/detail
- retell for 60–90 seconds after listening

## Phase 4 — Weeks 14–19
Target: ordinary conversational A2 speech.
- biography, housing, complaints, plans, leisure
- German subtitles only after first pass when needed
- first-pass target: 70% gist + key details
- second-pass target: 80%+

## Phase 5 — Weeks 20–24
Target: normal-speed practical German with less predictable content.
- university/admin/work/banking/transport/travel
- listen for intent, not every word
- record missed information types: numbers, names, verb tense, reason, outcome

## Phase 6 — Weeks 25–26
Target: timed A2 performance and B1-edge exposure.
- no subtitles on first pass
- timed questions
- delayed summary after audio ends
- first-pass target: >=70%

## Three-pass protocol
1. No pausing: gist + 3 facts.
2. Detail pass: answer task questions.
3. Transcript/subtitles: diagnose only; capture max 5 phrases.
4. Close source and retell aloud.

## Difficulty rule
If comprehension is below 60% after the second pass, step down in difficulty. If above 90% for three sessions, step up.

## Hub metric
Track first-pass comprehension separately from supported comprehension. Improvement in first-pass scores is the main listening-growth signal.

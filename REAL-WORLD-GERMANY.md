# Real-World Germany Missions

These missions convert classroom German into usable German. Do them in real life when practical; otherwise simulate faithfully in FunoGerman.

## Weeks 1–6
1. Introduce yourself aloud using name, origin, residence and language.
2. Ask one person a simple work/study/hobby question.
3. Order or buy one item using a full German sentence.
4. Ask or confirm an opening time/appointment phrase.
5. Describe one real person you know in German.
6. Give a 3-minute self-introduction without notes.

## Weeks 7–13
7. Describe your room/home and one housing problem.
8. Ask a direction or transport question.
9. Use a health/pharmacy sentence accurately.
10. Ask about price, size or product availability.
11. Invite someone or decline politely with a reason.
12. Complete one practical message entirely in German.
13. Handle a 5-minute mixed-topic conversation without a script.

## Weeks 14–19
14. Explain your education path.
15. Compare two real housing options or locations.
16. Practice a complaint/refund conversation.
17. Explain your study/career plans with reasons.
18. Recommend an event/place and explain why.
19. Complete a 5–7 minute A2 conversation with follow-up questions.

## Weeks 20–26
20. Ask one administrative/university question in German where practical.
21. Give a 60–90 second professional introduction.
22. Explain a payment/banking problem or inquiry.
23. Handle a transport delay/cancellation scenario.
24. Tell a travel story and give a recommendation.
25. Complete one whole routine interaction in German without switching to English unless necessary.
26. Final mission: 10-minute conversation covering identity, past experience, current life, plans, opinion and problem-solving.

## Mission scoring
5 = completed naturally with little help
4 = completed with some hesitation
3 = completed after one repair/retry
2 = heavily scripted
1 = attempted but communicative goal failed
0 = not attempted

Target average: 3+ by Month 2, 4+ by Month 6.

## Safety/practicality rule
Do not force strangers into language practice or delay staff in busy situations. Use ordinary interactions that you genuinely need. If no natural opportunity occurs, simulate with FunoGerman instead.

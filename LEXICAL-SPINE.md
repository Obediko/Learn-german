# German Lexical Spine — 26 Weeks

This file defines the active vocabulary architecture for the six-month programme. It prevents random word selection and makes spaced retrieval measurable.

## Active-vocabulary rules
- Weeks 1–8: target 12 new active items Monday–Friday.
- Weeks 9–26: target 10 new active items Monday–Friday.
- Saturday: no major new list; retrieve and repair.
- Every noun is learned with article + plural where useful + one natural phrase.
- Every verb is learned with infinitive + present form + one natural chunk; add Perfekt once introduced.
- Every adjective is learned with a contrast or collocation.
- Multiword chunks count as single learning units when they function as one communicative tool.
- An item becomes **active** only after successful delayed retrieval at 48+ hours and use in a self-generated sentence.

## Spaced-retrieval schedule
Same day → +1 day → +3 days → +7 days → +14 days → +30 days.

## Core chunk policy
From Week 6 onward, at least 40% of active learning items should be chunks, not isolated words. Examples:
- `Ich hätte gern …`
- `Könnten Sie das bitte wiederholen?`
- `Ich bin mir nicht sicher.`
- `Es kommt darauf an.`
- `Ich habe vor, …`
- `Was meinen Sie damit?`
- `Gibt es eine Alternative?`

## Retrieval standards
Weekly Saturday sample: 30 items.
- 10 from current week
- 10 from previous two weeks
- 10 older items

Score each item:
1 = recognized only
2 = recalled form/meaning
3 = used correctly in a new sentence

Target: >=80% of possible points.

## Adaptive load rule
If delayed recall is below 70% for three consecutive study days, reduce new-item load by 30% until delayed recall returns above 80%. Never solve weak retention by adding more words.

## Six-month target
Expect roughly 1,350–1,500 deliberately introduced active targets plus additional encountered vocabulary from input. The aim is not to memorize a dictionary; it is to build a highly reusable A2 lexical core with strong delayed recall and productive use.

# German Transfer Ladder — Use It in Germany

Every week ends with evidence that the language can transfer beyond exercises.

## Transfer levels
### Level 1 — scripted survival (Weeks 1–4)
Use prepared phrases for introductions, ordering, times and simple questions.

### Level 2 — constrained interaction (Weeks 5–8)
Handle family/housing/transport exchanges with one clarification or follow-up.

### Level 3 — routine independence (Weeks 9–13)
Manage pharmacy, shopping, invitations and simple past narration without English rescue.

### Level 4 — connected problem-solving (Weeks 14–19)
Explain biography, compare options, complain politely, justify plans and negotiate leisure choices.

### Level 5 — practical institutional German (Weeks 20–24)
Handle university administration, applications, banking, delays and travel problems.

### Level 6 — integrated independence (Weeks 25–26)
Respond to an unfamiliar but level-appropriate practical scenario with clarification, repair and follow-up.

## Weekly mission rule
Every Friday completes one actual interaction where naturally available; otherwise use a faithful Funo simulation. Never inconvenience strangers or staff merely to satisfy the course.

## Score 0–5
5 = natural completion with little help
4 = completed with hesitation
3 = completed after one retry/clarification
2 = heavily scripted
1 = attempted but failed to complete
0 = not attempted

Targets: average >=3 by Month 2, >=4 by Month 6.

## Evidence
Record one sentence after each mission:
- what I tried
- what worked
- where communication broke
- one phrase to reuse next time

The Hub should surface the next transfer mission prominently on Fridays and at weekly completion.

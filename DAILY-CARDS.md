# 156 Daily Lesson Cards

Each row below is one complete study card. There are 26 weeks × 6 days = **156 cards**. Use `DAILY-SESSION.md` for timing and `VIDEO-AUDIO-MAP.md` for resource codes.

**Daily vocabulary rule:** Mon–Fri learn 10–12 active items from the named theme; Saturday adds no major new vocabulary. Every noun = article + plural where useful; every verb = one natural phrase + past form once Perfekt begins. Retrieve yesterday + 3-day + 7-day items before adding new ones.

**Pass rule:** complete the output without notes at least once. If you fail, use `RECOVERY.md` and carry only the weak skill forward.

# Listening and Reading Track

## Listening progression
Weeks 1–4: slow beginner dialogue; identify names, numbers, times, basic actions.
Weeks 5–8: short everyday dialogues; gist + 3–5 details.
Weeks 9–13: pharmacy, transport, shopping, invitations; tolerate unknown words.
Weeks 14–19: A2 narratives, service interactions, plans and comparisons.
Weeks 20–26: routine authentic speech, announcements, interviews and problem-solving.

## Three-pass listening method
Pass 1: no transcript, no pause. Write the topic and 3 facts.
Pass 2: listen again and answer specific questions.
Pass 3: inspect transcript/subtitles only after trying independently. Extract no more than 5 reusable phrases.

Never turn a 3-minute audio into a 30-minute transcription exercise unless transcription is the day's explicit task.

## Reading progression
Weeks 1–4: profiles, menus, schedules, messages, signs.
Weeks 5–8: short adverts, housing listings, transport information.
Weeks 9–13: pharmacy/shop notices, invitations, short emails, basic web pages.
Weeks 14–19: biographies, housing comparisons, service complaints, event descriptions.
Weeks 20–26: university pages, job adverts, bills, transport disruption notices, travel texts and accessible news-style material.

## Reading protocol
1. Read title/headings first.
2. Predict purpose.
3. Read once without dictionary.
4. State the main message in German or simple English.
5. Answer detail questions.
6. Look up only words necessary for meaning or useful for active vocabulary.
7. Retell the text aloud in 3–6 sentences.

## Input balance
At least 3 input sessions per week:
- one Funo/Kurs-aligned listening;
- one VHS or Goethe-style task;
- one authentic/semi-authentic source such as Super Easy German or Easy German.

## Difficulty rule
Target material where roughly 80–95% is understandable from context. If comprehension is below 60% even after a second listen/read, move down in difficulty rather than grinding randomly.

## Saturday comprehension score
20 points total:
- gist 5;
- key details 8;
- inference/purpose 3;
- useful phrase extraction 2;
- oral retell 2.

## No-subtitle progression
Weeks 1–4: subtitles allowed after first pass.
Weeks 5–10: first two passes without English subtitles.
Weeks 11–18: German subtitles only after first pass.
Weeks 19–26: selected short clips must be attempted fully without subtitles before checking.

# Speaking and Pronunciation Track

## Principle
Speaking begins in Week 1. Do not wait until you have 'enough grammar'. Every week includes controlled speaking, spontaneous speaking, correction, immediate retry and one recorded sample.

## Fluency ladder
Weeks 1–2: 30–60 second answers.
Weeks 3–6: 2–3 minute topic responses.
Weeks 7–13: 4–5 minute practical conversations.
Weeks 14–19: 5–7 minute connected conversations with reasons and past events.
Weeks 20–26: 8–10 minute conversations including problem-solving, clarification and opinion.

## Weekly speaking pattern
Monday: 10 controlled sentences with target grammar.
Tuesday: pronunciation/shadowing + question-answer drill.
Wednesday: full FunoGerman conversation session.
Thursday: retell reading material aloud without reading.
Friday: real-world role-play or actual German interaction.
Saturday: recorded speaking test.

## Pronunciation progression
Weeks 1–2: alphabet, vowel length, ich-Laut, w/v/j.
Weeks 3–4: ö/ü, final devoicing, sentence stress.
Weeks 5–6: ch vs sch, r variants, unstressed endings.
Weeks 7–10: connected speech, separable verbs, stress in compounds.
Weeks 11–13: clause rhythm and verb-final sentences.
Weeks 14–19: speaking in chunks rather than word-by-word.
Weeks 20–26: intonation for clarification, politeness, disagreement and presentations.

## Shadowing protocol
1. Listen once for meaning.
2. Replay one sentence.
3. Mark the stressed word.
4. Speak simultaneously with the audio three times.
5. Record yourself once.
6. Compare rhythm first, individual sounds second.
7. Repeat only the weakest sentence.

## Correction priority
Correct in this order unless the week's target demands otherwise:
1. error changes meaning;
2. error blocks understanding;
3. repeated word-order/verb error;
4. target grammar error;
5. pronunciation error that affects intelligibility;
6. minor article/adjective error.

Do not interrupt every sentence. One high-value correction followed by immediate retry is better than a lecture.

## Speaking pass criteria
A weekly speaking task passes when the learner:
- completes the communicative goal;
- speaks without reading a full script;
- can repair one misunderstanding;
- uses at least 5 current-week items;
- uses the target grammar at least 3 times;
- does not stop for more than 5 seconds repeatedly.

## German-only zones
From Week 5: first 5 minutes of Wednesday speaking are German only.
From Week 10: first 10 minutes German only.
From Week 16 onward: all routine instructions and repair phrases should be German where possible.

## Essential repair phrases
- `Wie bitte?`
- `Könnten Sie das bitte wiederholen?`
- `Könnten Sie langsamer sprechen?`
- `Was bedeutet ...?`
- `Wie sagt man ... auf Deutsch?`
- `Ich meine ...`
- `Anders gesagt ...`
- `Einen Moment, ich überlege.`

These are survival tools, not optional vocabulary.

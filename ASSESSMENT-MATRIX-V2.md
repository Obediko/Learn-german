# German Assessment Matrix V2

The course tracks eight independent learning domains so one strong skill cannot hide another weak one.

## Domains
1. Vocabulary
2. Grammar
3. Listening
4. Reading
5. Speaking
6. Pronunciation
7. Writing
8. Real-world transfer

## Weekly evidence
- Vocabulary: delayed retrieval sample
- Grammar: production, not recognition
- Listening: first-pass + second-pass comprehension
- Reading: gist/detail/inference
- Speaking: timed spontaneous task
- Pronunciation: intelligibility sample
- Writing: communicative task
- Transfer: actual or faithful simulated real-world mission

## Weekly 100-point score
- Vocabulary 15
- Grammar 15
- Listening 12
- Reading 10
- Speaking 18
- Pronunciation 10
- Writing 12
- Transfer 8

## Interpretation
- 90–100: excellent; add stretch task only
- 80–89: strong; continue normally
- 70–79: pass; carry one repair target
- 60–69: fragile; reduce new load and repair
- <60: repeat targeted domain work before normal progression

No weekly pass if two or more domains are below 50%, regardless of overall score.

## Adaptive rule
The next week should assign one primary repair domain based on the lowest recent weighted trajectory, not the latest single mistake.

Examples:
- Listening <65% for two weeks → easier first-pass audio + three-pass protocol daily for 7 days.
- Speaking <65% → +10 min output daily and one additional Funo role-play.
- Pronunciation <65% → target one sound/rhythm feature + daily shadowing.
- Vocabulary <75% delayed recall → reduce new-item load 30%.
- Grammar <70% → 10 transformations + 5 spontaneous uses daily.
- Writing <70% → rewrite corrected version twice across 48h.

## Final bridge criteria
To begin B1.1 immediately:
- overall >=80
- speaking >=80% of speaking rubric
- writing >=80% of writing rubric
- delayed active vocabulary >=80%
- listening and reading each >=70%
- pronunciation >=75%
- transfer >=75%
- no domain <65%
- Perfekt and subordinate-clause word order functionally reliable

# Recovery and Catch-Up Protocol

The course should adapt to weak skills without collapsing the six-month schedule.

## Error codes
Tag every meaningful failure with one code:
- `VOCAB` — cannot retrieve needed word/chunk
- `GRAMMAR` — wrong structure blocks meaning or week's target
- `PRON` — pronunciation repeatedly harms intelligibility
- `LISTEN` — cannot extract gist/key details
- `READ` — cannot locate or infer required information
- `SPEAK` — cannot sustain output without English/script
- `WRITE` — cannot complete communicative task clearly
- `FLUENCY` — excessive pauses despite known language

## Repair rule
Do not repeat the whole lesson because of one weak domain.

For the next study day, use the first 10 minutes as a repair block:
- VOCAB: retrieve failed items, then use each in a fresh sentence.
- GRAMMAR: produce 10 correct examples + 5 transformations.
- PRON: shadow the failed phrase 5 times, record twice, choose the clearer take.
- LISTEN: replay one short segment at normal speed, then 0.75x only if necessary; retell without transcript.
- READ: reread for gist, then scan for 5 target facts; summarize in 3 German sentences.
- SPEAK: reduce task to 30-second chunks; repeat each chunk after one correction; reconnect.
- WRITE: rewrite only the failed sentences/section, then produce a new 5-sentence transfer version.
- FLUENCY: repeat the same 60–90 second topic three times, each time with fewer notes.

## Weekly score response
- **90–100:** strong. Add optional stretch input, but do not double vocabulary.
- **80–89:** on track. Continue normally.
- **70–79:** pass with one repair target next week.
- **60–69:** conditional pass. Carry two 10-minute repairs into Mon/Tue; Saturday retest.
- **50–59:** bridge week behavior. Reduce new vocabulary by 30% and spend the saved time on the two weakest domains.
- **<50:** do not load the next major grammar topic. Run a 3-day bridge using the failed week, then re-enter the schedule.

## Missed day rule
Never cram two full 60-minute lessons into one evening.
- Miss one day: continue next calendar day; move only the missed core production task into the next 10-minute repair block.
- Miss two consecutive days: Saturday becomes catch-up + test; no optional media.
- Miss 3+ days: restart from the last completed Saturday checkpoint, but preserve vocabulary already mastered.

## Vocabulary overload rule
If active retrieval falls below 70% for two consecutive sessions:
1. stop adding new items for one day;
2. retrieve failed items in phrases;
3. cut the next two days' new quota from 10–12 to 6–8;
4. return to normal quota only after ≥80% retrieval.

## Speaking protection rule
Speaking is never removed to make room for catch-up. On the busiest day, keep at least 10 minutes of spoken production.

## German-in-Germany rule
A failed real-world interaction is still a successful attempt if you:
1. initiated in German;
2. understood at least the core response or asked for clarification;
3. logged the phrase you needed but lacked;
4. replayed the situation correctly later with FunoGerman.

## Monthly gate failure
If a CEFR checkpoint fails:
- isolate weak domain(s);
- run 3–6 bridge sessions;
- retest only failed domains plus one integrated speaking task;
- do not label yourself at the next level until the productive evidence exists.

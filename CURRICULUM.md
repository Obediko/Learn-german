# 26-Week German Curriculum

**Dates:** 14 September 2026 to 13 March 2027
**Core target:** strong A2, with a B1.1 bridge only if mastery gates are passed.
**Backbone:** FunoGerman master curriculum aligned to Kurs DaF A1/A2 and Goethe-style outcomes.

## How to use this file
Each week has six study days. Use `DAILY-SESSION.md` for the 60-minute structure. Saturday is always assessment + repair, not a normal new-content day.

## Weeks 1–6 — A1.1 foundations

### Week 1 — Herzlich willkommen: identity, greetings, spelling
Grammar: ich/du/Sie; sein; heißen; kommen; wohnen; W-questions; basic word order.
Vocabulary: greetings, countries, languages, alphabet, numbers 0–20, personal details.
Pronunciation: German alphabet, ch in ich, initial w/v/j, long vs short vowels.
Monday: learn 12 active items; build 10 self-introduction sentences; say name, origin, residence, language.
Tuesday: retrieve vocabulary; spelling drill; listen to beginner greetings; ask and answer 10 personal questions.
Wednesday: 15-minute Funo speaking role-play: meeting a new person; correction-retry loop.
Thursday: read two short profiles; write a 50-word profile; transform statements into questions.
Friday: Super Easy German beginner input; shadow 60 seconds; simulate registering your name/email/address.
Saturday: 2-minute self-introduction recording + vocabulary test + mini writing test.

### Week 2 — Studium, Arbeit und Freizeit
Grammar: regular present tense; haben; gern/nicht gern; simple yes/no questions.
Vocabulary: study, research, work, hobbies, weekday activities, common verbs.
Pronunciation: final consonants, word stress, verb endings.
Monday: 12 items; describe what you do and five things you like.
Tuesday: present-tense conjugation drill; listening on hobbies; sentence transformations.
Wednesday: conversation: Was machst du gern? Was studierst/arbeitest du?
Thursday: read a short timetable/profile; write 60 words about your week.
Friday: real-life transfer: ask someone one simple question about work/study/hobby if practical, otherwise simulate in Funo.
Saturday: 3-minute speaking test + 20-item retrieval.

### Week 3 — Essen und Trinken
Grammar: accusative foundations; möchten; nehmen; kein/nicht; plural awareness.
Vocabulary: meals, drinks, quantities, supermarket and restaurant phrases.
Pronunciation: ö/ü, r in common words, compound stress.
Monday: 12 items; learn chunks: Ich hätte gern..., Ich nehme..., Sonst noch etwas?
Tuesday: menu listening; role-play ordering and paying.
Wednesday: Funo restaurant conversation without English prompts.
Thursday: read menu/offer texts; write a shopping list plus 8 full sentences.
Friday: real-world mission: order or buy one item in German using a full sentence.
Saturday: restaurant simulation + dictation of prices/quantities.

### Week 4 — Uhrzeit, Alltag, Termine
Grammar: separable verbs; sentence bracket; time expressions; inversion after time-first openings.
Vocabulary: clock time, daily routine, appointments, days, common separable verbs.
Pronunciation: rhythm of sentence bracket; unstressed prefixes.
Monday: describe a normal day in 10 sentences.
Tuesday: listen for times and appointments; write what you hear.
Wednesday: speaking: arrange a meeting and negotiate time.
Thursday: read a timetable; write an 80-word daily routine.
Friday: real-world mission: ask/confirm an opening time or appointment phrase in German.
Saturday: timed routine narration + separable-verb test.

### Week 5 — Familie und Freunde
Grammar: possessives mein/dein/sein/ihr; adjective basics in predicate position; ages/jobs.
Vocabulary: family, relationships, appearance/personality, occupations.
Monday: family vocabulary + 10 possession sentences.
Tuesday: listening: who is who; pronunciation of ich/ach sounds.
Wednesday: describe 3 people without notes.
Thursday: read a family profile; write 80 words about family/friends.
Friday: Funo conversation on relationships and where people live/work.
Saturday: A1.1 speaking rehearsal.

### Week 6 — A1.1 consolidation and checkpoint
No major new grammar.
Monday: repair weak grammar from Weeks 1–5.
Tuesday: vocabulary cumulative retrieval: target 80%+.
Wednesday: 5-minute spontaneous conversation covering identity, routine, food, family.
Thursday: reading + writing checkpoint.
Friday: Goethe-style A1 listening/reading sample practice.
Saturday: A1.1 checkpoint. Pass requires speaking, writing, listening/reading, and core present-tense control.

## Weeks 7–13 — A1.2 and A1 exit

### Week 7 — Wohnen am Studienort
Grammar: es gibt; accusative; common dative location chunks; in/auf/bei; basic two-way-preposition awareness.
Vocabulary: rooms, furniture, rent, housing features, housing problems.
Monday: describe your room/home.
Tuesday: housing-ad listening and key-detail extraction.
Wednesday: role-play asking about a room/apartment.
Thursday: read two listings; write 80-word housing request.
Friday: real-world mission: identify and say 5 housing-related sentences about your actual accommodation.
Saturday: housing speaking + reading test.

### Week 8 — Unterwegs in der Stadt + first Perfekt
Grammar: basic Perfekt with haben/sein; frequent participles; war/hatte chunks.
Vocabulary: transport, city places, directions, yesterday/weekend language.
Monday: directions + transport phrases.
Tuesday: Perfekt build: Ich habe ... gemacht; Ich bin ... gefahren/gegangen.
Wednesday: tell what you did yesterday in 8–10 sentences.
Thursday: read a route/transport notice; write a short past account.
Friday: real-world mission: ask a direction or transport question in German, or simulate precisely.
Saturday: route role-play + 90-second past narration.

### Week 9 — Gesundheit
Grammar: müssen/sollen/können; imperative chunks; mir ist...; ich habe Schmerzen.
Vocabulary: body, symptoms, pharmacy, doctor, advice.
Monday: symptom phrases.
Tuesday: listening at pharmacy/doctor.
Wednesday: role-play patient/pharmacist.
Thursday: read medicine/appointment-style information; write a short sick message.
Friday: practice asking for something at a pharmacy in German.
Saturday: health scenario test.

### Week 10 — Shopping and clothes
Grammar: dieser/diese/dieses awareness; adjective colour/size phrases; comparative basics.
Vocabulary: clothes, sizes, colours, price, exchange/return phrases.
Monday: product description.
Tuesday: listening to price/size choices.
Wednesday: role-play buying/returning an item.
Thursday: compare two products in writing.
Friday: real-world shop interaction or simulation.
Saturday: shopping test.

### Week 11 — Feste, Einladungen, Gründe
Grammar: weil; verb-final introduction; modal review; dates.
Vocabulary: celebrations, invitations, accepting/declining, reasons.
Monday: invitation phrases.
Tuesday: weil sentence transformations.
Wednesday: invite, accept, decline, explain why.
Thursday: read an invitation; write a reply.
Friday: voice-message simulation to invite someone.
Saturday: weil + invitation assessment.

### Week 12 — A1 integration
Grammar: full A1 repair: present, modals, accusative/dative chunks, separables, Perfekt, weil.
Monday: grammar diagnostic.
Tuesday: listening diagnostic.
Wednesday: 6-minute conversation covering three topics.
Thursday: 80–100 word practical message.
Friday: Goethe A1 model-task practice.
Saturday: mock A1 exam sections.

### Week 13 — A1 exit gate
Monday–Friday: targeted repair only. No content inflation.
Saturday final gate: spontaneous exchange, past narration, practical writing, listening and reading. If two domains remain below 50%, repeat targeted bridge work before full A2 load.

## Weeks 14–19 — A2.1 connected everyday communication

### Week 14 — Bildungsweg und Lebenslauf
Grammar: Perfekt productive review; Präteritum of sein/haben/modals; temporal connectors zuerst/dann/danach/später.
Vocabulary: school, university, qualifications, stages, experiences.
Tasks: narrate education history; read a biography; write 100–120 words about your path.
Saturday: 4-minute connected biography.

### Week 15 — Wohnungssuche and comparisons
Grammar: comparative/superlative; dative/accusative in practical housing contexts; relative description without full relative-clause load.
Vocabulary: requirements, landlord contact, utilities, advantages/disadvantages.
Tasks: compare two flats, call/email a landlord, explain preferences.
Saturday: housing decision task.

### Week 16 — Shopping, service and complaints
Grammar: polite requests with könnte/würde gern; adjective/quantity consolidation.
Vocabulary: defects, receipts, refund/exchange, customer service.
Tasks: complaint role-play; write a concise complaint email.
Saturday: service-recovery scenario.

### Week 17 — Plans and future
Grammar: werden + infinitive for explicit future; wollen/vorhaben; weil/dass review.
Vocabulary: career/study plans, goals, decisions, reasons.
Tasks: explain 6-month and 3-year plans; write 120 words.
Saturday: future-plan presentation.

### Week 18 — Kultur, Sport, Freizeitangebote
Grammar: wenn for conditions/time; recommendations; comparisons.
Vocabulary: events, tickets, recommendations, preferences, invitations.
Tasks: choose among events and justify; invite someone and negotiate.
Saturday: recommendation task.

### Week 19 — A2.1 checkpoint
Four-skill checkpoint: 5-minute conversation, 120-word text, A2 listening/reading tasks, vocabulary retrieval and grammar production.

## Weeks 20–25 — A2.2 practical independence

### Week 20 — Universität und Orientierung
Grammar: indirect-question chunks; clarification language; prepositions in campus contexts.
Vocabulary: offices, documents, enrolment, appointments, clarification.
Real-world mission: ask one administrative question in German where practical.

### Week 21 — Bewerbung und Praktikum
Grammar: formal email structure; weil/dass/wenn accuracy; past experience language.
Vocabulary: CV, skills, responsibilities, availability, interview phrases.
Tasks: 60-second professional introduction; short application email.

### Week 22 — Geld und Banking
Grammar: quantities, comparisons, polite requests; conditional chunks with wenn.
Vocabulary: account, transfer, fee, budget, invoice, payment.
Tasks: explain a payment problem; read a bill; write an inquiry.

### Week 23 — Verkehr, Verspätung, Alternativen
Grammar: subordinate clauses under pressure; deshalb/trotzdem; modal verbs for options.
Vocabulary: delay, cancellation, platform, connection, alternative route.
Tasks: handle a delayed journey; ask staff for alternatives; narrate what happened.

### Week 24 — Reisen, Regionen, Erlebnisse
Grammar: connected past narration; adjective/comparison review; sequencing.
Vocabulary: accommodation, landscape, travel problem, recommendation.
Tasks: 5-minute travel story; written recommendation.

### Week 25 — A2 integration and pressure week
Monday: grammar diagnostic.
Tuesday: vocabulary cumulative retrieval.
Wednesday: 8-minute spontaneous conversation.
Thursday: 150-word formal/practical text.
Friday: Goethe A2-style listening/reading practice.
Saturday: full A2 mock checkpoint.

## Week 26 — Final assessment and B1 bridge decision
Monday: repair weakest domain.
Tuesday: final vocabulary test.
Wednesday: 10-minute speaking recording covering past, present, plans, opinion and problem-solving.
Thursday: 150–180 word writing task.
Friday: final listening/reading simulation.
Saturday: final score and decision.

### B1 bridge criterion
Begin B1.1 immediately only if all are true:
- overall final score >= 80/100;
- speaking >= 20/25;
- writing >= 12/15;
- A2 vocabulary retrieval >= 80%;
- subordinate clauses and Perfekt are functionally reliable;
- no domain below 65%.

Otherwise spend 4–6 weeks consolidating A2 before accelerating.

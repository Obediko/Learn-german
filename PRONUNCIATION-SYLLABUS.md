# German Pronunciation Syllabus — 26 Weeks

Pronunciation is trained for intelligibility, not accent erasure. The sequence is sound → word → phrase → spontaneous speech.

## Phase 1 — Weeks 1–6: core sound map
- alphabet and spelling
- long vs short vowels
- ä / ö / ü
- ich-Laut vs ach-Laut
- initial w / v / j / z
- final devoicing: b/p, d/t, g/k
- German r awareness
- word stress and common endings

Weekly task: 30–60 seconds of shadowing plus one self-recording.

## Phase 2 — Weeks 7–13: connected beginner speech
- consonant clusters
- sch / ch / sp / st
- compound stress
- separable-verb rhythm
- modal-verb rhythm
- sentence stress
- question intonation
- reduced unstressed syllables
- clause-final verb rhythm with weil

Weekly task: 60–90 seconds shadowing + 2–3 minute spontaneous speech.

## Phase 3 — Weeks 14–19: fluent chunks
- linking across word boundaries
- rhythm in past narration
- contrastive stress
- polite request intonation
- chunking longer noun phrases
- natural pauses and breath groups

Weekly task: 90 seconds shadowing + 4–5 minute speaking sample.

## Phase 4 — Weeks 20–26: pressure and repair
- clarification intonation
- disagreement without sounding abrupt
- formal vs informal register rhythm
- presentation pacing
- self-correction without losing flow
- maintaining intelligibility under speed and stress

Weekly task: 5–10 minute spontaneous speaking sample at checkpoints.

## Scoring dimensions (0–5 each)
1. vowel/consonant intelligibility
2. stress and rhythm
3. phrase chunking
4. intonation
5. repair after mispronunciation

Target by final week: average >=4/5 with no dimension below 3.

## Correction priority
Meaning-breaking sound → repeated intelligibility problem → target sound of the week → rhythm/stress → minor accent detail.

Never interrupt fluent speech for every small pronunciation issue. Correct one high-value problem, explain briefly, then require immediate retry.
